This report presents a summary of the work carried out as part of my final-year project entitled "Modernization of an Integrated Management Tool for Accounting Services" at Tamtam International, in fulfillment of the requirements for the engineering degree in Networks and Information Systems (IRSI) at the Faculty of Sciences and Techniques of Marrakech, Cadi Ayyad University.

The goal of this project is to modernize an integrated management tool designed to monitor accounting services, tailored to an ecosystem adapted to economic and legal professions.

My work focused on two main sections: on the one hand, facilitating the consultation of processed services, and on the other, preparing monthly billing through the implementation of a neutralization mechanism. This includes the generation of monthly service reports, the configuration of collaborators' seniority levels, and the setup of OAM codes.

For project management, we adopted the Agile SCRUM methodology, dividing the work into sprints. The implementation relies on modern web technologies to ensure project quality, namely PHP with the Symfony framework for the backend, React.js for the frontend, and MySQL as the database management system.

\textbf{Keywords:} SCRUM, Integration, Import and synchronization, Hexagonal architecture

\textbf{Technologies :} PHP, Symfony, ReactJS, Redux, MySQL
